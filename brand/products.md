# Murree Sparkletts: product facts (draft)

Source: https://murreebrewery.com/sparkletts/ (read 2026-11-05).

## About
- Premium bottled drinking water, sourced from the foothills of the Himalayas.
- Plant in Hattar, KPK. Owned by Tops Food and Beverages since 2011. New filling lines added in 2015.
- Certifications: ISO 9001 and HACCP.
- Current website tagline: "The new style of sensation with Ease & Beauty."
