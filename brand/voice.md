# Murree Sparkletts Brand Voice

**Brand idea:** Murree Sparkletts is premium Himalayan drinking water and the lifestyle that goes with it. Line: *Drink Healthy, Live Healthy.* Website tagline: "The new style of sensation with Ease & Beauty."

**Facts we can state:** sourced from the Himalayan foothills, filled at the plant in Hattar, KPK, owned by Tops Food and Beverages since 2011, new filling lines added in 2015, ISO 9001 and HACCP certified.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Premium, healthy, trustworthy.** Calm and confident, never loud. Warm, but never casual or slangy. Short, clear sentences.

## 2. Words we use

pure Himalayan, premium, Himalayan foothills, fresh, crisp, clean, hydration, lifestyle, ease, beauty, ISO 9001 and HACCP certified, Drink Healthy, Live Healthy.

## 3. Words we avoid

cheap, discount, bargain, chemical, medicine, cure, detox, miracle, "the best in the world", and any talk that puts down competitors.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- That the water cures, treats or prevents any illness, or promises health results such as living longer.
- Purity or "contaminant-free" claims without a lab report to back them. When we make one, name the lab and the report date.
- "The best", "number one" or "one of the best tested" without proof. Say what was tested and by whom instead.
- Any comparison with named competitor brands.
- ISO 9001 or HACCP unless the certificates are current.
- "Pure Himalayan" for anything other than water sourced from the Himalayan foothills.
