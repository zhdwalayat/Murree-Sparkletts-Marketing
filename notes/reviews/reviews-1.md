# Practice reviews, file 1 of 6

Made-up reviews of the earlier Murree Sparkletts Sampler Box. Practice data only. These are not real customers.

**1. ★★★★★ (5/5)** Hina S., Islamabad
The 750 ml glass bottle was the star of the box. It looked lovely on the dinner table, and the water tasted crisp and cold straight from the fridge.

**2. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)** Kamran T., Karachi
It took eleven days to reach Karachi. When it came, one of the 500 ml bottles had a cracked cap and had leaked onto the card inside.

**3. ★★★★☆ (4/5)** Nadia F., Rawalpindi
I liked trying all the sizes before committing. Four stars only because there was just one Alkaline bottle, and that was the one I wanted more of.

**4. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)** Usman A., Peshawar
The water is fine, with a clean taste. The box itself was squashed on one corner and the outer sleeve was torn.

**5. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)** Saima K., Multan

**6. ★★★★★ (5/5)** Faisal M., Lahore
Fresh, light taste. The 330 ml bottles fit perfectly in my gym bag. I've already asked about a monthly box of those.

**7. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)** Rabia Z., Faisalabad

**8. ★★★★☆ (4/5)** Omar J., Islamabad
Good mix. I'd swap the 1.5 L for a second glass bottle, though. We finished that in one evening.

**9. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)** Mehwish B., Hyderabad

**10. ★★★★★ (5/5)** Zain H., Rawalpindi

**11. ★★★★☆ (4/5)** Sadia N., Lahore

**13. ★★★★★ (5/5)** Fatima D., Karachi
