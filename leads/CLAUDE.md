# Rules for the leads folder

- These are made-up practice people. They are not real customers.
- Never add real people or real email addresses.
- Every row needs a consent value.
- Never change the @example.com addresses. That domain can never receive mail.

## Groups

- `everyday-households`: families and homes who want clean, fresh drinking water every day.
- `gift-and-event-buyers`: people buying for gifts, offices, weddings or events.
- `active-lifestyle`: people who care about hydration, fitness and a healthy routine.
