# Campaign Plan: Murree Sparkletts Launch to the Waitlist

Status: draft. Items marked **(confirm)** need the team's decision.

## 1. Campaign Overview

- **Name:** Drink Healthy, Live Healthy: the Murree Sparkletts launch
- **Summary:** Introduce premium Himalayan drinking water to our waitlist with four emails over four weeks, so members start a Murree Sparkletts subscription **(confirm the offer)**.
- **Primary goal:** 300 waitlist members start a subscription between Monday 2026-12-07 and Sunday 2027-01-03.
- **Secondary goals:** grow clicks to the Murree Sparkletts website, and learn which group responds best.
- **Budget:** small. Email only, no paid ads.

## 2. Target Audience

About 2,000 people who joined the waitlist, in three groups.

| Group | Who they are | What they care about | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| water-curious | Beginners new to the brand | What makes Murree Sparkletts different, and whether to trust it | Awareness |
| gift-buyers | People buying for guests, family or an office | Presentation, reliability, an easy way to order | Consideration |
| daily-drinkers | People who already buy bottled water every day | A premium everyday choice that is simple to keep stocked | Decision |

They all joined the waitlist themselves (consent: waitlist form), so they already know the name.

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:** Murree Sparkletts is premium Himalayan water for everyday life. *Drink Healthy, Live Healthy.*

Supporting messages, using only facts from `brand/voice.md`:

1. **Where it comes from.** Sourced from the Himalayan foothills.
2. **Where it is filled.** Filled at our plant in Hattar, KPK, owned by Tops Food and Beverages since 2011, with new filling lines added in 2015.
3. **How it is made.** ISO 9001 and HACCP certified **(confirm both certificates are current before this is sent)**.
4. **How it feels.** Fresh, crisp, clean. Hydration with ease and beauty.

Products we can mention (from `brand/products.md`): 330 ml, 500 ml, 750 ml glass (size to be checked, one link says 770 ml), 1.5 L, 12 L, the 19 L "Big Bottle" for home and office dispensers, and Alkaline. Give no prices, and no mineral or pH details, until the client supplies them.

Tone: calm, confident and warm. Short sentences. Sign-off: "Stay hydrated, The Murree Sparkletts Team".

## 4. Channels

| Channel | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|

| Website launch page | The one place every email links to (`https://murreesparkletts.example/launch`, placeholder) | Medium |

No paid, social or PR work in this plan.

## 7. Success Metrics

- **Primary:** 300 subscriptions started by 2027-01-03. This is 15% of 2,000 recipients, which is high for email. We should set a lower fallback target **(confirm)**.
- **Secondary:** click rate to the launch page, open rate, unsubscribe rate (aim for below 0.5% per send), clicks by group, number of subscriptions by group.
- **Tracking:** the sending tool's reports, plus a tagged link per email. Record results in `reports/`.
- **Cadence:** a check the day after each send, and a final report in the first week of January.

## 8. Budget

Small, and email only. The main costs are the team's time and the sending tool. Keep about 10% of the budget as contingency.

## 9. Risks

1. **The goal is too high.** Mitigation: agree a fallback target, and read results after Email 1.
2. **Claims go beyond what we can prove.** Mitigation: every email is checked against `brand/voice.md`. No purity, health or "best" claims, and no named competitors.
3. **The holiday period lowers attention.** Emails 3 and 4 land on 22 and 29 December. Mitigation: keep them short, with one clear link.
